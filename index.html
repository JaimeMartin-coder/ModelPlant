Teacher notes

Purpose: Students use a multi-scale model to follow the reactants and products of photosynthesis from the whole plant down to the chloroplast.

NGSS three dimensions

  • DCI: LS1.C Organization for Matter and Energy Flow in Organisms
  • CCC: Energy and Matter; Scale, Proportion, and Quantity; Systems and System Models
  • SEP: Developing and Using Models
  • Performance expectation touched: HS-LS1-5 (model of how photosynthesis transforms light energy into stored chemical energy)

Suggested use

  • Have students predict first: “What goes in? What comes out? Where?” Then reveal with the arrows.
  • Each screen has a “Think about it” question (good for a quick write or turn-and-talk) and a “Going further” extension.
  • Misconceptions addressed: plants get food from soil; plants only photosynthesize and do not respire; the oxygen comes from CO₂ (it comes from water).

Differentiation

  • ELL: every moving item has a word label, new terms have tap-to-define popups in plain language, and any explanation can be read aloud.
  • SPED: pause button and speed slider, the chloroplast screen is stepped one idea at a time, and Reading Options offers dyslexia-friendly spacing, text size, and page color.
  • Gifted: “Going further” prompts push on scale estimates, water loss, ATP/NADPH cycling, and phloem transport.

Accessibility note

Meaning never relies on color alone. Each flow has a text label, and active steps use thicker solid outlines.
